## ⚡ What I've shipped

- **📚 A documentation platform that updates itself** — from GitLab MR to production:
  AI-driven updates of **600+ pages** (FR & EN), automated screenshots, human validation
  via MR before publish. Time-to-live cut from **days → under one hour**.
- **🔁 50+ n8n workflows, 10K+ executions a year** — accounting, IT compliance, monitoring,
  product data. Manual tasks deleted across Product, SI and HR.
- **💰 A quote generator, end to end** — product design (needs, mockups, user testing),
  then development, launch and run. **30 pre-quotes generated in the first month.**
- **🤖 AI-assisted development** — LLM agents, MCP servers, automated dev harnesses:
  the tooling builds the tooling.
- **📊 Decisions on facts, not opinions** — Metabase & Grafana watching tool adoption,
  usage, availability and gains across the whole ecosystem.
- **🧭 Delivery at scale** — rituals, roadmap and risk for **5 teams / ~40 people**,
  coaching Product Owners, and the delivery tooling (Jira, Tuleap, Miro) to match.
